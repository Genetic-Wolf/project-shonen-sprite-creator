PROJECT SHONEN SPRITE CREATOR v0.5.0 — MULTI-OUTPUT ASSET WORKFLOW

CORE CHANGE
A reusable piece now has ONE logical identity and may have several visual representations:

TV        Walking/map sprite
FG        Face/portrait generator artwork
TVD       Downed/damaged representation
SV        Side-view battle artwork
Variation Generator/library preview

Example:
PS-HAIR-04217 — Long Ponytail
  TV         COMPLETE
  FG         COMPLETE
  TVD        MISSING
  SV         IN PROGRESS
  Variation  COMPLETE

The artist does NOT create five unrelated assets. They select "Long Ponytail" and add/edit whichever representation is needed.

RULES
1. Never scale TV artwork to make FG or SV art.
2. Every representation is authored against its own canonical master.
3. All representations keep the same logical asset ID.
4. Missing representations are allowed during production and are shown as incomplete.
5. TV-only characters remain usable while richer outputs are being produced.
6. The program/library owns technical filenames and IDs.
7. Artists work with normal names and categories.

CLIP STUDIO
Each output template remains a normal layered art workspace concept:
REFERENCE/GUIDES + BASE REFERENCE + DRAW THIS OUTPUT.
The artist may save a .CLIP copy. Transparent PNG remains the validated interchange/export format.

NEXT PRODUCTION GATE
Before bulk SV/FG/TVD artwork begins, the corresponding canonical base pose artwork must be artist-approved and then locked just like the TV geometry.
